Account & Data Deletion

TurtleReporter — Wave Labs · Universidade da Madeira

App ID: com.turtles.turtlereportertitanium

Last updated: August 3, 2026

1. Delete Your Account From Inside the App

The fastest way to delete Your Account and Personal Data is directly in the TurtleReporter application:

  1. Open TurtleReporter and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. The action cannot be undone.

Deletion is immediate. Your authentication account and Your Personal Data are removed from Our systems, and You are signed out of the application.

2. Request Deletion Without the App

If You can no longer access the application, You may request deletion by email. Send a message from the email address registered on Your Account to:

noel.ferreira@staff.uma.pt or team@wave-labs.org

Please include the subject line "TurtleReporter — Account Deletion Request" and the email address associated with the Account. We verify ownership before deleting and respond within 30 days.

3. What Is Deleted and What Is Kept

DataWhat happens
Name, email address, profile photoDeleted permanently
Authentication account (Firebase) and session tokensDeleted permanently
Language and application preferencesDeleted permanently
Link between You and Your trips and sightingsDeleted permanently
Data stored on Your device (offline queue, cached session)Deleted when the account is removed or the app is uninstalled
Scientific sighting records: species, count, date, coordinates, sea and sky conditions, sighting photographsRetained in anonymised form, with no link to Your identity

Why sighting records are retained: TurtleReporter is a citizen-science service. Sighting observations form part of a scientific dataset on sea turtle distribution used for research and conservation by Universidade da Madeira. Once Your Account is deleted these records can no longer be attributed to You, directly or indirectly, and are therefore no longer Personal Data.

4. Retention Periods

  • Personal Data: deleted immediately on request. Encrypted backups containing Personal Data are rotated out within 90 days.
  • Anonymised scientific data: retained indefinitely for research purposes.
  • Server access logs: retained for up to 12 months for security purposes, then deleted.
