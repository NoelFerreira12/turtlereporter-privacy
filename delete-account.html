Account & Data Deletion

TurtleReporter — Wave Labs · University of Madeira

App ID: com.turtles.turtlereportertitanium

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open TurtleReporter and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. The action cannot be undone.

Deletion is immediate: Your Account and Personal Data are removed from Our server and You are signed out.

The guest mode has no personal account to delete.

2. Request deletion without the app

If You can no longer use the app, send an email from the address registered on Your Account to noel.ferreira@staff.uma.pt or team@wave-labs.org, with the subject "TurtleReporter — Account Deletion Request".

We confirm that the request comes from the owner of the Account before deleting, and reply within 30 days.

3. What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Name, email address, password, profile photoDeleted
Session tokens and, for accounts created before October 22, 2026, the Firebase authentication recordDeleted
Language and application preferencesDeleted
Link between You and Your trips, sightings and survey answersDeleted
Data on Your device (offline queue, saved session)Deleted when the Account is removed or the app is uninstalled
Scientific records: species, number of animals, date, coordinates, trip routes, sea and sky conditions, sighting photographs, survey answersKept in anonymised form, with no link to Your identity
Name and email typed in a sighting report form (these are stored with the report, also when no account is used)Kept with the report so that researchers can contact the observer; deleted on request by email (section 2)

Why scientific records are kept: TurtleReporter is a citizen-science service. Sightings form part of a dataset on sea turtle distribution used for research and conservation by the University of Madeira. Once Your Account is deleted these records are no longer linked to You.

4. Retention

  • Personal Data: deleted when You delete the Account or when We act on Your request.
  • Backups and server logs: kept only for as long as needed for security and recovery, and then deleted.
  • Anonymised scientific data: kept for research purposes.
