Privacy Policy

At a glance

  • We collect your name, email and password to run your account, and the sightings, photos and trip routes you choose to record.
  • Your location is used to place each sighting on the map and to record the boat's route only while a trip you started is active — including with the screen off, with a persistent notification. It stops when you end the trip.

  • You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete Account) — see Account & Data Deletion.

1. Definitions

  • Application means the TurtleReporter software provided by the Company and downloaded by You on any electronic device.
  • Company ("the Company", "We", "Us" or "Our") refers to Wave Labs, ITI/LARSyS, ARDITI, Polo Científico e Tecnológico da Madeira, Caminho da Penteada, piso -2, 9020-105 Funchal, Portugal, which develops the Application together with the University of Madeira.
  • Country refers to Portugal.
  • Account means a unique account created for You to access the Service.
  • Personal Data is any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Service refers to the Application.
  • You means the individual accessing or using the Service.

The Application is used to record sea turtle sightings for conservation and scientific research.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address and password, provided when You register. The password is stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash. You may also add a profile photo.
  • Sightings, reports and trips: the information You enter (species, number of animals, date and time, sea and sky conditions, notes), the photographs You attach and the position of each sighting.
  • Location: see section 3.
  • Photographs: taken with the camera or chosen by You from the Device, only to attach them to a sighting or report or to set a profile photo. The Application does not read Your photo library beyond the photos You select.
  • Preferences: application language and settings, kept on the Device and in Your Account.
  • Usage Data: collected automatically when the Application communicates with Our server (IP address, device and system information sent with each request, access logs), used only for security and troubleshooting.

If You use the guest mode, no name or email is collected; trips and sightings are recorded without an Account.

3. Location

With Your permission, the Application uses the Device's precise GPS position:

  • During a trip You start: the route of the boat is recorded about every 10 seconds. Recording continues when the Application is in the background, the screen is off or the Application has been closed, through a foreground service that shows a persistent notification. Recording stops when You end the trip.
  • When You create a sighting or report: the position is read once to georeference it.
  • While the Application is open: the position may be read to show where You are on the map or to help You choose a point; it is not stored for this purpose.

No location is collected in the background when there is no active trip. The name of the trip's starting place is obtained from the Device's geocoding service. Positions are sent to Our server as part of the scientific record.

4. How we use your data

  • To provide and maintain the Service, including creating and authenticating Your Account.
  • To record and display Your sightings, trips and routes.
  • To support scientific research on sea turtles by the University of Madeira, using the sighting records and routes.
  • To keep the Service secure and working properly.
  • To send You service emails You request, such as a password reset link.

We do not sell Your Personal Data, do not share it with business partners and do not use it for marketing or advertising.

5. Where your data is stored

Your Account, sightings, photographs, trips and GPS positions are stored on the project's server, hosted by the University of Madeira in Portugal and operated by the project team. All communication between the Application and the server uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) only.

Older accounts: Accounts created before October 22, 2026 were registered with Firebase Authentication (Google LLC). For these Accounts, Firebase may still hold the email address and an authentication record until the Account is deleted.

Third-party services: maps are displayed with Google Maps, and place names are obtained from the Device's geocoding service, both subject to Google's privacy policy. Password reset emails are delivered through Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France).

International transfers: these providers may process data on servers outside Portugal or the European Union, under the safeguards of the GDPR, including standard contractual clauses.

6. Retention

Personal Data is kept while Your Account is active. When the Account is deleted, Personal Data is removed from Our systems; sighting records may be kept in anonymised form, with no link to Your identity, because they are part of a scientific dataset. Details are on the Account & Data Deletion page.

7. Account deletion and your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase Your Personal Data, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD).

Delete Your Account in the Application: Profile → Delete Account. This removes Your Account, Your profile photo and Your sessions from Our server and, for older Accounts, the Firebase authentication record.

You may also request deletion, or exercise any other right, by contacting Us (section 12). We reply within 30 days. Full details: Account & Data Deletion.

Notifications

9. Disclosure and security

Security: We apply appropriate technical measures (encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access control), but no method of transmission over the Internet or of electronic storage is 100% secure.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under the age of 13, and We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under 13. If You are a parent or guardian and believe Your child has provided Us with Personal Data, please contact Us and We will delete it.
